# Global instructions

## Language

Write all replies to me in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Use short sentences. Maximum 20 words in procedures, 25 in descriptions.
- Give one instruction per sentence.
- Use the active voice.
- Use approved words in their approved meaning only.
- Avoid jargon, slang, and idioms.
- Use the present tense where possible.
- Keep articles ("a", "the") in place.

Keep code, commands, file paths, and quoted output unchanged.
